# Job Wars arena prompts

Use the built-in image generation tool. Attach [Job Fair Arena](raw/arena/market.png) as the reference, then generate one full-resolution image per district. Use the shared prompt followed by the district's **District** line. The filename in each heading is relative to `art/raw/arena/`.

## Shared prompt

Generate one finished square, opaque, full-bleed background for Job Wars, an original hand-painted 2D mobile RPG. Use the attached Job Fair arena as a strict camera, geometry, framing and painting reference. Retheme the entire setting as described below. Keep the same centered, straight-on elevated 3/4 viewpoint, no rotation or isometric diagonal. Preserve the reference floor perimeter and its position: back edge around 23% from top, front edge around 81%, side boundaries gently splay outward toward viewer, tall back wall plus low continuous left, right, and front boundaries. All four inner edges clearly readable and uninterrupted. Large flat playable floor spanning most of the image, quiet medium-light matte texture and faint low-contrast center roundel if appropriate. All decorative objects and their major shadows are confined to the back wall or beyond the perimeter. Match the reference's chunky warm-brown outlines, richly painted texture, clear shapes and soft upper-left lighting. Distinct recognizable district, cohesive with the reference. No characters, no objects on the playable floor, no UI, lettering, logos or watermarks. One square scene.
